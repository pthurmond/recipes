# Baked Potato Soup - A Houlihan's Copycat Recipe

This is based on the classic Houlihan's Potato Soup Recipe. Derived and modified from the recipe found on [cdkitchen](https://www.cdkitchen.com/recipes/recs/517/Houlihans_Baked_Potato_Soup46110.shtml).
This is my own take on the recipe. I prefer to use golden potatoes, leaving the potato skin on. I also prefer to include white pepper, garlic powder, and bacon crumbles directly in the soup. I also prefer it to be thicker. So it includes less water and more potato flakes.

## Ingredients

- 3 pounds Golden (or baking) potatoes
- 1/2 pound butter
- 4 cups diced yellow onion
- 2/3 cup flour
- 6 cups water
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken base
- 3 cups instant potato flakes
- 2 teaspoons dried basil
- 1 teaspoon Tabasco sauce
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons sea salt
- 2 teaspoons black pepper
- 1 teaspoon white pepper
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 cups milk
- Grated Cheddar cheese, for topping
- Sliced scallions, for topping
- 1 cup crumbled bacon, cooked until crisp

## Directions

- Wash and prick the potatoes.
- Bake in a preheated 400°F oven until a fork easily pierces to the center. About 60-70 minutes.
- Remove the potatoes from the oven and let them cool completely.
- Melt the butter in a large saucepan.
- Add the onions to the melted butter.
- Sauté over low heat for 10 minutes, or until the onions are translucent. Do not let the onions burn.
- Add the flour to the onions and butter.
- Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring well, until the flour is absorbed.
- In a separate container, combine the water, chicken base, potato flakes, basil, Tabasco, garlic powder, sea salt, black pepper, and white pepper.
- Stir or whisk thoroughly to eliminate lumps.
- Slowly add the mixture to the onion-butter mixture, stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
- Increase the heat to medium. Cook until the soup begins to gently simmer.
- Add the milk and heavy cream. Stir until smooth and lightly thickened.
- Simmer for 15 minutes. Do not boil; keep the soup at a light simmer.
- Cut the potatoes into 1/2- to 1-inch cubes and Set the potatoes aside.
- Add the cubed baked potatoes.
- Stir to combine.
- Remove the soup from the heat.
- Serve topped with grated Cheddar cheese, sliced scallions, and crumbled bacon.

## Possible Experiments

I'm curious to know how this would do if you replace the butter with extra virgin olive oil (and significantly less) as well as use coconut milk instead of heavy cream and milk and then forego the cheese and bacon to make this entirely vegan. Though you might increase the salt to account for what would come from the dairy and bacon sources.
