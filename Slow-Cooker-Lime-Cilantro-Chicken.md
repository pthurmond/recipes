# Slow Cooker Cilantro Lime Chicken

## Ingredients
* 1 jar (16 ounces / 454 g) salsa
* 1 package (1.25 ounces / 35 g) dry taco seasoning mix
* 1 medium lime, juiced
* 3 tablespoons (45 ml) chopped fresh cilantro
* 3 pounds (1.36 kg) skinless, boneless chicken thighs

## Directions
1. Gather all ingredients.
2. Stir the salsa (16 ounces / 454 g), dry taco seasoning mix (1.25 ounces / 35 g), the juice from 1 medium lime, and chopped fresh cilantro (3 tablespoons / 45 ml) together in a slow cooker until well combined. 
3. Add the skinless, boneless chicken thighs (3 pounds / 1.36 kg) and spoon the salsa mixture over the top to coat.
4. Cover and cook until the chicken is no longer pink in the center and the juices run clear, on Low for 6 to 8 hours or High for 4 hours. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read at least 165 degrees F (74 degrees C).
5. Shred the chicken in the slow cooker with two forks, then mix with the sauce.
6. Enjoy!
